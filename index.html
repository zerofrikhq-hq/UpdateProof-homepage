COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about what UpdateProof does and does not do.

How does visual regression diffing work without breaking site performance? expand_more
In the free tier, page checks evaluate DOM status codes and headers locally. In the Pro tier, visual snapshots are rendered via an external headless Playwright container. The browser emulation runs entirely outside of your WordPress server's PHP execution thread, meaning visitor load is completely unaffected.
Where are screenshots and verification histories stored? expand_more
UpdateProof uses a local-first evidence paradigm. Verification histories, timestamps, and page diff logs are stored securely in your WordPress database or your designated private worker bucket. We do not harvest or resell your clients' internal site data.
Does UpdateProof replace my existing backup plugin or rollbacks? expand_more
No. UpdateProof is an automated verification suite, not a snapshot backup utility or hosting rollback engine. You should always maintain a standard backup system like UpdraftPlus, BlogVault, or your hosting provider's daily image snapshots. UpdateProof tells you when a rollback is actually needed.
Can I customize or white-label the client reports? expand_more
Yes. Agency tier licenses include complete white-label options allowing you to embed your agency logo, custom domain, and customized client disclaimers while suppressing UpdateProof branding from exported customer PDFs or HTML artifacts.
