Questions, answered plainly

Designed to keep the important parts visible.

Does UpdateProof update my site automatically?

No. The Free workflow requires you to create a plan and explicitly approve plugin or theme changes. Pro scheduled checks collect evidence; they do not apply updates automatically.

Does it send my site content to an AI service?

No. The Free plugin stores its safety evidence locally and does not transmit PHP source code, post content, or visitor data.

Does Pro include backup and restore today?

Not yet. The current Pro preview records recovery readiness and blocks approval until you confirm a tested restore point. A real provider integration will be added only after it is tested.

Who is UpdateProof for?

Freelancers, agencies, developers, and site owners who want a simple maintenance record before and after updates.
