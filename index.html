Questions, answered plainly

Designed to keep the important parts visible.

Does UpdateProof update my site automatically?

No. The Free workflow requires you to create a plan and explicitly approve plugin or theme changes. Pro scheduled checks collect evidence; they do not apply updates automatically.

What happens after I pay for Pro?

Razorpay confirms the annual subscription, the licensing service verifies the payment, and the popup shows your license key plus a secure temporary download link. You also receive the license email.

Does Pro include backup and restore?

Not yet. UpdateProof records recovery readiness, but you should continue using your hosting backup or backup provider. A real integration will be added only after it is tested.

Who is UpdateProof for?

Freelancers, agencies, developers, and site owners who want a simple maintenance record before and after updates.
